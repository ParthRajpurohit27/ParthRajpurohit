The Long Version

I'm a 12th-grade student specializing in Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and Informatics Practices (PCM + IP) at Orion International School, Bhopal. My obsession with technology and structural programming started before most of my classmates owned a smartphone.

In Standard 5, I wrote my first web interfaces using only MDN documentation and YouTube tutorials — no bootcamps, no courses, no hand-holding. That habit of learning directly from primary sources stuck. Everything I've built since has been self-directed, problem-first, and deployed to real users.

Beyond code, I bootstrapped a real e-commerce business from zero capital — handled PAN & video KYC, integrated payment gateways, negotiated logistics pipelines, and shipped physical orders across six states. That experience taught me systems thinking in a way no textbook ever could.

I'm continuously adding to my technical stack: currently deepening expertise in prompt engineering, cybersecurity fundamentals, and scalable JavaScript architectures. When I'm not coding, I'm probably optimizing something that didn't need optimizing.
